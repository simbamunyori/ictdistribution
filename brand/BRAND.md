# ICT Distribution Africa brand guide

**Tagline:** The right technology, sourced and delivered.
**About the name:** Pan-African ICT distribution.

## The idea
Six units stacked into a forward arrow: stock moving with purpose from source to customer. One unit in Amber leads, the signal of a delivery on its way. Ink and Steel give the weight of serious infrastructure; Amber and Cobalt bring energy and clarity to commerce.

## Logo
- `logo/ictdistribution-lockup-ink.svg` on light backgrounds, `logo/ictdistribution-lockup-white.svg` on dark.
- `logo/ictdistribution-mark.svg` where space is tight; `logo/ictdistribution-mark-tile.svg` for avatars and social profiles.
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of one unit of the mark. Minimum width: 120 px for the lockup, 24 px for the mark.
- Never recolour, stretch, outline, add effects or place the logo on a busy image.
- All lettering is converted to outlines, so the files work without fonts installed.

## Colour
| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0D1117` | primary |
| Amber | `#F2A30F` | brand |
| Steel | `#5C6673` | support |
| Cobalt | `#2D5BE3` | accent |
| Fog | `#F2F4F6` | support |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | support |

Use the accent sparingly: one element per screen at most. Text on light backgrounds uses Ink; on dark backgrounds, white.

## Type
- **Display:** Inter Display, SemiBold or Bold, tight tracking (-2%), for headlines and the wordmark.
- **Text:** Inter Regular and Medium for interface and body copy.
- **Numbers and code:** JetBrains Mono.
All are free, open-licence fonts from Google Fonts or rsms.me.

## Voice
Plain, confident and short. No exclamation marks, no em dashes, no jargon.

## Files
- `logo/` SVG logos, `png/` exports and app icons (16 to 1024 px), `app-icon/` source icons, `tokens/` colour and type tokens for code (CSS and JSON).
